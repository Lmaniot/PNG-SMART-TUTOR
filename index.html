About PNG Smart Tutor AI

This is a single-file, browser-only intelligent tutor designed for Papua New Guinea secondary schools. It generates lessons that follow the Standards-Based Curriculum (SBC) and Outcomes-Based Education (OBE) framework used by the PNG Department of Education.

The tutor's defining feature is subject adaptation. When you select Physics, it teaches like a Physics teacher — formulas, units, free-body diagrams. When you select English, it teaches like a Language teacher — grammar, comprehension, structured writing. The same engine reshapes itself for ICT, Maths, Biology, Chemistry, Business Studies, Social Science, and every other PNG syllabus subject.

Lessons are produced through the Groq API. You provide your own free Groq key (stored only in your browser) so the app stays free to host on Netlify with no backend.

Built single-file in HTML, native CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Designed for PNG classrooms — including low-bandwidth and mobile-first use.

Groq API Key
